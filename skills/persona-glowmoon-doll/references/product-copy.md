## § PART 2 — Listing Structure

Every Glowmoon doll listing should follow this information hierarchy.
Order matters — collectors scan for specs quickly; lore rewards those who read.

### Standard structure

```
1. CHARACTER HOOK / LORE (2–4 sentences)
   — Who is this doll? What is their story?
   — Omit for accessories; lead with function instead.

2. WHAT YOU'RE GETTING (1–2 sentences)
   — Blank or full set? Resin color options? Faceup included or not?
   — Be explicit. Ambiguity erodes trust.

3. SPECIFICATIONS (structured list)
   — All required fields (see Part 3)

4. FACEUP / CUSTOMIZATION NOTES
   — Blank: encourage customization; note faceup artists are welcome
   — With faceup: describe the faceup style briefly
   — Custom faceup requests: direct to email

5. RESIN COLOR NOTE
   — Always include the screen variance disclaimer
   — Note any specific color behavior for this resin

6. CARE AND MAINTENANCE (brief)
   — Key points only; link to care page if one exists

7. POLICIES (brief)
   — Returns, shipping exceptions, wait times if applicable
```

### Listing length guidance
- **Blank doll**: Medium length — let the specs and lore carry it. Faceup
  artists are experienced; don't over-explain.
- **Full set**: Longer — describe outfit, accessories, faceup style; buyers
  want to know what they're getting in detail.
- **Accessories**: Short — function first, compatibility second, charm third.

## § PART 3 — Required Specifications

Every doll listing must include all of these. Missing specs are the most
common cause of collector hesitation. Present as a clean list, not prose.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Height** | In cm; e.g., "16 inches (approx. 40cm)" |
| **Material** | "Polyurethane resin" — always spell out |
| **Scale** | 1/3, 1/4, etc. — enables clothing shopping |
| **Head circumference** | In cm — critical for wig sizing |
| **Eye size** | In mm — critical for eye shopping |
| **Neck circumference** | In cm — for hybrid compatibility |
| **Chest / waist / hip** | In cm — for clothing compatibility |
| **Foot length** | In cm — for shoe shopping |
| **Wig size** | Standard size designation (e.g., 7-8") |
| **Joint type** | Single or double-jointed; ball-and-socket locations |
| **Resin color(s) available** | List all options; name them clearly |
| **Blank or with faceup** | State explicitly; if both, note price difference |
| **What's included** | Itemize: doll only / with eyes / with wig / full set contents |

Note on Boudi: Boudi is described as 16 inches on the site. Confirm
exact cm measurement and all other specs with Isabel during intake —

## § PART 4 — BJD Community Copy Conventions

These are the established norms for how legitimate artist doll listings read.
Deviating signals inexperience; following them signals belonging.

### Language that builds legitimacy
- Use correct terminology: faceup (not "makeup"), blank (not "unpainted"),
  restring (not "string replacement"), head-cap (not "removable head")
- Name the scale explicitly (1/3, 1/4, etc.) — collectors use this for
  accessory shopping
- State measurements in centimeters — this is the community standard
- Reference what size wig, eyes, and clothing fit — removes a major friction point
- Note whether the doll can stand unaided — poseability is a purchase factor
- If the doll is cast in small batches or by hand: say so. It's a feature,
  not an apology.

### Language that signals mass-market or inexperience (avoid)
- "Super cute" / "adorable" / "precious" — reads as playline, not artist
- Vague scale: "medium-sized doll" instead of the actual scale designation
- No measurements at all — immediate red flag for experienced collectors
- Describing the faceup as "makeup" — marks the seller as unfamiliar with
  the hobby
- "Perfect for display" — fine, but if it's also poseable, say so
- Missing resin color disclaimer — collectors know resin looks different
  on screens; omitting the disclaimer reads as inexperienced or deceptive

### Resin color disclaimer (always include, in your own words)
Resin colors vary between monitors and real life. Photos are taken under
controlled lighting and may not perfectly represent the color you receive.
Isabel's existing language ("resin colors may appear different on monitors")
is correct and community-standard — keep using it or a variation of it.

### The blank doll buyer
Faceup artists and experienced customizers represent a significant share
of blank doll buyers. They do not need to be sold on the hobby — they need
specs, surface quality notes, and the knowledge that the resin takes paint
well. For this audience, the listing should convey:
- That the resin surface is suitable for faceup work
- Whether any sanding or prep is expected from the buyer
- That the artist respects the customizer's vision (don't prescribe how
  to paint it)

### The full set buyer
This buyer wants to display or photograph the doll without additional work.
They want to know exactly what they're receiving — every included accessory,
the faceup style, whether eyes/wig are included, and the character context.
Lead with lore, close with a complete itemized list.

## § PART 5 — Character Lore Integration

Glowmoon's strongest differentiator in the artist doll market is
character-driven design. Rune is the clearest example: she has a name,
backstory, family, and world. This is direct appeal to OC-motivated collectors.

### Rules for lore copy
- The lore should be evocative, not exhaustive — a sketch, not a novel
- Leave room for the collector's interpretation and projection
- Don't lock down every detail — "she lost her parents to a mysterious
  accident" is better than over-explaining the accident
- The lore should feel emotionally resonant, not world-buildy or clinical
- Avoid named references to other media/IP; keep it original

### Rune — existing lore (from site, preserve and build from)
Rune is a little witch looking for her place in the world. She lost her
parents to a mysterious accident and went to live with her enigmatic
grandmother, an ancient witch with untold power who also dallies in rose
grafting and stray cat collecting. While Rune loves living with and learning
from Grandma Silverfrost, she is determined to do whatever it takes
(including selling the odd illegal potion) to find and reunite with her parents.

Usage note: This lore leads the Rune product listings. Different Rune
editions (Seattle Grunge, etc.) are wardrobe/styling variations of the
same character — the lore stays consistent, the styling copy changes.

### Edition-specific copy (for Rune variants)
Each Rune edition needs:
- Edition name in title: Rune "[Edition Name]"
- Brief styling note: what does this outfit/look say about Rune right now?
- Tie back to character: even a grunge outfit can feel true to who Rune is
- Keep it short — the lore page or main Rune listing carries the full story

### For future characters (Boudi and others)
Boudi is described as "the original Glowmoon Doll" — a blank resin BJD
for customizers. The current listing is sparse on lore. Options:
1. Keep Boudi lore-light and position as the "artist's canvas" (valid —
   some collectors prefer a blank slate for their own characters)
2. Develop Boudi's character similarly to Rune
Confirm Isabel's intent during intake before writing Boudi lore copy.

```
**Specifications**
- Height: [X]cm / [X] inches
- Scale: [1/3 / 1/4 / other]
- Material: Polyurethane resin
- Resin colors available: [list]
- Head circumference: [X]cm
- Eye size: [X]mm
- Wig size: [size]
- Neck: [X]cm
- Chest / Waist / Hips: [X]cm / [X]cm / [X]cm
- Foot length: [X]cm
- Joints: [single / double] ball-and-socket at [list locations]
- Can stand unaided: [yes / with sueding / no]
- Includes: Doll body only / [list extras]

**Faceup**
This doll arrives blank. Faceup commissions can be arranged separately —
reach out by email if you'd like a custom faceup from Isabel.
[Or: Currently I offer artist-choice faceups in dark or light brow options.
For unusual brow colors or custom requests, email me.]

**Resin color note**
Resin colors may appear slightly different in person than they do on your
monitor. Photos are taken under controlled lighting to represent color
as accurately as possible.

**Care**
Clean with room-temperature water and a soft cloth with a small amount
of non-abrasive soap. Avoid prolonged sun exposure to slow resin yellowing.
[Link to full care page if it exists]

**Shipping and returns**
Returns accepted — email to get started and we'll find a solution together.
International shipping available on a case-by-case basis; reach out before
ordering.
```
